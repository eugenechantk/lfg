### 2026-11-05 — Chat send contract drift broke shares to chats

**What happened:** The share extension (Oct 23) sends `{text, clientId}`. Durable chats (Nov 3-4) added a chat route on the same path that needs `{text, requestID}`, replies in another shape, and refuses uploads. Every share to a chat failed with "Enter valid a message identifier". Eugene found it on his phone.
**Why this matters:** Two send paths now share one URL with different contracts. The chat work updated the app composer and missed the extension.
**What better looks like:** When a new session kind gets its own API, grep every caller of the old one (`sendMessageRequest`, `uploadRequest`, the share extension, desktop) in the same change, and add a test per caller. A route that serves two contracts should fail loudly on the wrong body shape.
