### 2026-08-30 — FlowDeck `device list` reports stale USB devices

**What happened:** `flowdeck device list` showed three USB-connected devices (Donna's iPhone, iPad Mini, Hihi) plus Eugene's iPhone on network. `xcrun devicectl device info files` failed with "unable to locate a device" for every USB entry; only the network iPhone was actually reachable (it returned a lock error, proving connectivity). Two wasted probe attempts.
**Why this was wrong/inefficient:** FlowDeck's list appears to come from xcodebuild destinations, which caches previously-seen devices; CoreDevice reachability is the ground truth for file/log operations.
**What better looks like:** For devicectl file transfers, don't trust FlowDeck's connection column — probe the device directly and treat "unable to locate" as "not actually connected." A lock error means the device IS reachable.
