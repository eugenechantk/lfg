### 2026-10-10 — mosh-bridged tunnel bootstrap failures left no trace in mosh-bridge.log

**What happened:** Eugene hit "mosh-server bootstrap on 'pro' failed: Connection to UNKNOWN port 65535 timed out". The log had only the LAN sweep line at 16:43:20; the failed tunnel attempt was shown in the iTerm window and nowhere else. Diagnosis had to infer it from a missing "carrier started" line. By the time I probed, the path was healthy (15/15 connects at ~0.7s), Pro's tunnel log was clean, and the cause of the >5s stall could not be found.
**Why this matters:** A transient failure with no timestamped record can't be correlated with anything after the fact.
**What better looks like:** Every failure path in a connect script logs rc + elapsed time. Done this session: mosh-bridged now logs each tunnel attempt and retries once on ssh exit 255. Also learned: "Connection to UNKNOWN port 65535 timed out" = ssh ConnectTimeout firing while a ProxyCommand (cloudflared) hasn't delivered the banner.
