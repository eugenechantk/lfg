### 2026-07-18 — Unsafe port-kill in my verification rig hit ANOTHER agent's app

**What happened:** My auditor rig brief said to go offline via `lsof -ti :8799 | xargs kill`. Simulator LFG apps hold CLIENT TCP sockets on :8799, so that also killed the test app AND another lfg agent's app (pid 41986) connected to the same throwaway server. Auditor recovered with `lsof -ti :8799 -sTCP:LISTEN | xargs kill` (server only).
**Why it matters:** Root CLAUDE.md already says "kill by PORT" for restarts, but `lsof -ti :PORT` returns BOTH listeners and connected clients. On a shared host with concurrent agents, killing clients is collateral damage. Caused a real hit to another agent.
**Better / memory-worthy:** To kill only a server, ALWAYS scope to the listening socket: `lsof -ti :PORT -sTCP:LISTEN`. Never a bare `lsof -ti :PORT | xargs kill` on a shared host.
