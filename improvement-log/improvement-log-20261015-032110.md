### 2026-10-15 — `safeAreaPadding` with no length adds 16pt

**What happened:** The S2 composer and panel sat 16pt higher than the boards. I logged window and container insets (34 / 0), tried a container-diff offset (no effect), then logged the chrome's own frame before reading the modifier's meaning: `.safeAreaPadding(.bottom)` with a nil length adds the system default padding (16pt) to the safe area; it does not pad *by* the safe area.
