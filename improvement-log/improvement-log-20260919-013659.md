## Log

### 2026-09-19 — One invalid flag failed a 12-cookie login
**What happened:** The first retry after the `reason` change reported `SameSite=None requires Secure.` WebKit keeps such cookies (Apple sets one); `validateTransfer` threw for the whole transfer, so 0 of 12 reached Chrome. The reason plumbing found it in one attempt after an hour of blind theorizing.
**What better looks like:** Validation of a batch should degrade a single odd cookie (drop the attribute Chrome would refuse) rather than reject the login. Both server and phone export now do that.

### 2026-09-19 — Sign-in delivery failures were undiagnosable by design
**What happened:** Noto session 56c91326 got `failed, installed 0, total 11` for developer.apple.com and told Eugene apple.com was not in the extension's allowed list. Chrome prefs showed `https://*/*` granted since before the failure, and a pre-login transfer of 6 Apple cookies installed fine. The extension stops at the first `cookies.set` rejection and reports only a count; the server, CLI, history and phone all drop the cause.
**Why this was wrong:** The `request-phone-sign-in` skill's `failed` line said "check extension permissions", so the agent reported the only hypothesis it was handed. A pipeline that cannot say *which* cookie was refused guarantees wrong diagnoses.
**What better looks like:** Every adapter result now carries `reason` (`permission-missing:<origin>`, `set-rejected:<name>@<domain>:<browser message>`, `busy`, `timeout`, `browser-disconnected`, or the transfer error), sanitized server-side, persisted in history, printed by the CLI, shown on the phone and in the extension status. Skill text tells agents to read it. Rule: when a failure path collapses to a boolean, add the reason before theorizing.
