## Direction (Eugene, 2026-10-23)

List first, modelled on the share sheet of a messaging app he sent: "Send to", search pill, pinned New session row, Recent (by lastUserAt) and All sessions as grouped cards with round checks. Picking a session raises a bottom panel: preview, the text field (exactly what is sent; starts with the link), who gets it and what Send will do, Send. One session per share. Blue check (S2 accent), not green.

Rows: 1 entry + list + search + chosen + editing + New session + in the session (waiting in the strip, then a bubble); 2 quote / screenshots / PDF / not sent; 3 After Send confirmations; 4 to 6 the first draft (A compose first, B pick first), superseded.

## Code facts the design relies on (checked 2026-10-23)

- Send: `POST /api/sessions/:id/send {text, clientId}`; files: `POST /api/sessions/:id/upload` (64 MB), paths appended one per line after the text.
- Closed sessions resume through the same send call. New session: `POST /api/sessions/new {cwd, prompt, agent, model}`.
- Busy or prompt-waiting sessions: the message waits in the pending strip; busy turns absorb it mid-turn.
- No App Group, no keychain access group, no share extension, no INSendMessageIntent today. Hosts live in `UserDefaults.standard` (`lfg.hosts`); Cloudflare Access tokens in the Keychain (`dev.omg.lfg.cloudflare-access`); outbox in GRDB `lfg-store.sqlite`.
- `lfg://session/<id>` deep link exists (Open in LFG).
