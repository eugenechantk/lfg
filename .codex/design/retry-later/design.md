## Why the first version felt apart

`TaskRetryLaterSheet` is a generic grouped `Form` with five separate problems:
- a task-name row;
- a segmented picker;
- a bare text field;
- `.bordered` preset buttons that never show which one is picked;
- a "Retry at" row in a separate section at the bottom.

The confirm action is a small toolbar word, "Schedule retry". On iPad and Mac the fixed sheet leaves half its height empty. The failed card stacks three equal-weight buttons.

The task screens already have a language for picking a time: the edit form's When card (`ScheduleEditorSection`). The redesign uses that card and adds no new parts.

## Direction: the When card, made compact

### Failed card (`TaskStatusCard`, kind `.failed`)
- **Buttons:** keep the board's two, Retry now (prominent) and Ask to fix (secondary). They are unchanged.
- **Retry later…:** replace the third full-width `actionButton(.retryLater, prominent: false)` with a plain tertiary button. It uses Label "Retry later…" with the `clock` icon.
  - Style: `TaskDetailMetrics.button` size (16 phone, 13 Mac), semibold, `TaskColors.accent`.
  - Layout: leading-aligned, min height 44 on the phone, between the buttons and the divider and footnote.
- **Mac:** same order.

### Retry scheduled card (`TaskStatusCardModel.make`, `retryAt != nil`)
- **Card fill:** neutral, `TaskPalette.card`; the failure is handled.
- **Label:** "RETRY SCHEDULED" in `TaskColors.accent`, with a 14pt clock icon.
- **Main and sub:** main is `f.dayAndTime(at)` ("Today, 1:36 PM"); sub is `"\(f.fullDate(at)) · \(f.countdown(to: at))"`.
- **Actions:**
  - Primary: Retry now (prominent).
  - Secondary: "Change time" with a clock icon. Drop the "…".
  - Tertiary: "Cancel retry", a plain button, 16 medium, `TaskColors.grey`, min height 44.
- **Footnote:** "One extra run. Regular schedule unchanged: next run tomorrow, 2:00 AM". Without an active schedule: "One extra run. The regular schedule stays unchanged."

### Sheet layout (phone, iPad, Mac)

Top to bottom:

1. **Header:** Cancel at top left (cancellation action). The centred title is "Retry later" (17 semibold), with the task name below it (12, `TaskColors.grey`, one line).
   - When `retryAt` already exists, the title is "Change retry time".
   - There is no confirmation item in the toolbar.
2. **When card:** one rounded card. The fill matches the edit form's grouped section (`secondarySystemGroupedBackground`; dark #2C2C2E on a #1C1C1E sheet, light #FFFFFF on #F2F2F7). Radius 20 on the phone, 12 on the Mac. Inside:
   - **Tabs:** "In minutes" | "At a time", the same control as `ScheduleEditorSection.modePicker`. That is the native `.segmented` on iOS and `TaskSegmentedPicker` on the Mac, with 34 / 28pt height and 12pt inset.
   - **Sentence:** the preview, so the user always sees the actual time before confirming.
     - Phone: 21 semibold (Mac 16 / 650), "Retry in 45 minutes".
     - Line below: 14 (Mac 12), `TaskColors.grey`, "Today at 1:36 PM · Sunday, Oct 18". It refreshes every 15 seconds, as `TimelineView` does now.
     - At a time: "Retry tomorrow at 9:00 AM" over "Monday, Oct 19 · in 20 h 9 min".
   - **Chips (In minutes):** "35 min", "45 min", "1 hour", "Custom".
     - Style: `TaskChipButtonStyle(selected:unselectedFill:)`, the frequency chips; 34 / 26pt, 6pt apart, 16pt (Mac 14) side inset. Unpicked chips use the idle fill (`color-decision.md` §3), never a grouped background.
     - Picked: filled `TaskColors.accentFill` with white text. A typed value of 35, 45 or 60 selects its chip.
   - **Custom row** (only when Custom is picked), divider above: "Minutes from now" on the left, then a `TaskNumberField` and "min" on the right.
     - Field width 72 on the phone, so "525600" fits (the edit form's is 64); 56 on the Mac.
     - Picking Custom focuses the field.
   - **At a time rows:** "Date" and "Time", each with a divider above.
     - Phone: native compact `DatePicker`s, which draw their own idle fill with accent text.
     - Mac: `TaskDatePill` and `TaskTimePill`, the same as the Once form.
     - Range: future only.
   - **Time zone line:** "Hong Kong time (GMT+8)", 12 (Mac 11), `TaskColors.grey`, divider above. It shows the device's local zone, like `ScheduleEditorSection.zoneLine` but without "Change".
3. **Footnote** under the card: 13 (Mac 11), `TaskColors.grey`. "Runs once more at this time. The regular schedule stays as it is. {host} must be awake; if it’s asleep, the retry runs when it wakes."
4. **Error line** (only after a failed save): 14 (Mac 12), `TaskColors.danger`, directly above the button. "Couldn’t reach {host}. Your choice is kept. Check the connection and try again." Server error text may replace the first sentence.
5. **Primary button:** a full-width capsule.
   - Phone: 50pt, `TaskColors.accentFill`, 17 semibold white, in `.safeAreaInset(edge: .bottom)`, so it stays above the keyboard and the home indicator.
   - Mac: a bottom-right row of Cancel (28pt, `TaskPalette.control`) and the primary (28pt, `accentFill`). Return confirms (`.defaultAction`); Esc cancels (`.cancelAction`).
   - Label names the time: "Retry at 1:36 PM"; "Retry tomorrow at 9:00 AM" for another day; "Retry Tue, Oct 20 at 9:00 AM" beyond that.
   - Saving: ProgressView and "Scheduling…"; the form is disabled at 0.55 opacity, with `interactiveDismissDisabled`.
   - Invalid: disabled, with the idle fill (light `tertiarySystemFill`, dark #3A3A3C) and a `systemGray` label reading "Retry later". The capsule stays visible.

### Sizing
- **Phone:** the sheet hugs its content, not a fixed or medium detent. Measure the content height and use `.presentationDetents([.height(h)])`, with `.large` as a second detent for the keyboard.
  - Board heights: 476pt for presets, 526pt with the custom row or with At a time, plus 46pt with the error line.
  - Use `presentationBackground` to match the edit sheet.
- **iPad:** a form sheet sized to fit the content (`.presentationSizing(.fitted)`, about 420pt wide). No empty lower half.
- **Mac:** 360pt wide over the 400pt narrow window, with a 16pt inset and 12pt gaps; content height (about 330pt for presets). Replace `.frame(width: 480, height: 420)`.

### Validation and behaviour (unchanged rules)
- **Whole minutes:** 1 to 525,600 (`ScheduledTaskRetryDraft.scheduledDate`). An invalid entry turns the field border and the sentence's line `TaskColors.danger`. The line then reads "Enter 1 to 525,600 whole minutes", the sentence reads "Retry in … minutes", and the button disables.
- **Exact time:** must be in the future. Otherwise the line reads "Choose a time in the future".
- **Retry semantics:** one extra run. The recurring settings and run counts don't change. A sleeping host retries when it wakes.
- **Network error:** keep every input and show the error line.
- **Default when opening from a failed card:** In minutes, "1 hour" picked (today's default is 60). The board shows "45 min" picked to demonstrate the selected state.
- **Opening from Change time:** At a time, with the saved date and time filled in.

### Accessibility and identifiers
- **Traits:** chips get `.accessibilityAddTraits(.isSelected)` when picked. The sentence and its line form one combined element, announced on change.
- **Button label:** carries the time, so VoiceOver confirms it.
- **Targets:** at least 44pt on the phone, including the tertiary text buttons.
- **Keep these identifiers:** `task_retry_later`, `task_change_retry_time`, `task_cancel_retry`, `task_retry_sheet`, `task_retry_mode`, `task_retry_minutes`, `task_retry_preset_35/45/60`, `task_retry_date`, `task_retry_preview` (the sentence block), `task_retry_validation`, `task_retry_error`, `task_retry_dismiss`, `task_retry_schedule`.
- **Add:** `task_retry_preset_custom` and `task_retry_time`, for the time picker if it is split from the date.

## Limitations
- The boards are web mock-ups of native controls. Use native `.segmented`, `DatePicker`, `TaskSegmentedPicker` and the pills; don't match the boards pixel for pixel.
- The "Change retry time" title, iPad, and the Mac light At a time state aren't drawn as separate boards. The rules above cover them.
- The model chip draws the Claude logo with the alias "Opus" as sample data.
- "Now" in the boards is fixed at Sunday, Oct 18, 12:51 PM, Hong Kong time.
